Excel file (.xlsx / .xls)
Reads the "DC file" sheet. Columns (any order): OP N0., Date, Customer Name, Vertical, Customer Category, Region, Company, Sales Person, FAE Person, Segment, Project, Existing MPN, Existing Make, Suggested MPN, Suggested Make, Project Status, Sample Qty, Samples stage, Status Month, EAU Qty, QPS, Unit Price in USD, Currency, Potential, PP date FAE, MP date Sales, Product team remarks, Sales Team Remark, FAE Team remark, Remarks. A row is matched to an existing line by Company + OP No + Customer Name → it UPDATES that line (so a changed Suggested MPN / price / status updates, no duplicate). Blank cells keep the old value. New OP/Customer = inserted. Several part-lines under one OP+Customer are matched in row order.
Default Company (used when the sheet has no Company column)
